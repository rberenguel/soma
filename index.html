Soma Cube by Ruben Berenguel

The goal is to assemble the seven pieces into a solid 3x3x3 cube or one of the other included puzzle shapes.

There are 240 unique solutions to the classic cube. When you solve the cube, you can export the solution. The export is an image that contains a 2D "unfolded" view of the solved cube's faces with the printed unique signature of this solution. This file is named with your solution's index number (1-240) and its unique signature, creating a shareable record of your find. Can you find them all? For other puzzles, a picture of the solved puzzle is generated.

Incorporates the extra Soma puzzles from Thorleif Bundgård's Soma "fan" site. As I add more puzzles I will try to add references to their authors.

Controls:

  • Select/Place: Click a piece to select it. Drag to place it, they magically try to snap to where you may want them.
  • Orbit Camera: Drag the background. Also deselects pieces.
  • Zoom: Use the mouse wheel or pinch-to-zoom.
  • Rotate Piece: When a piece is selected, use the A, R, S or Z, X, V keys, or the rotation trackball that appears on the bottom right.
